FAQ

Common Questions

How do I know my donation is being used properly? ▼
TSF maintains full financial transparency. Every donation is recorded, tracked, and reported. We publish regular impact reports and our Finance Manager ensures strict accountability. You can view donation allocation on our Donate page.
Can I donate from outside Ghana? ▼
Absolutely! We accept international donations via Visa/Mastercard and Google Pay. International supporters are a vital part of our community — every contribution makes a difference regardless of where it comes from.
How can I volunteer with TSF? ▼
Select "Volunteer" in the contact form above and tell us your skills, availability, and what area you'd like to contribute to. We welcome mentors, educators, tech professionals, healthcare workers, and administrators.
How does TSF select children to support? ▼
Children are identified through our community coordinators, school partnerships, and referrals from trusted community leaders. We assess financial need, academic potential, and family circumstances before enrolling a child in our programs.
Can organisations partner with TSF? ▼
Yes! We actively seek partnerships with schools, tech companies, healthcare organisations, and community groups. Select "Partnership" in the contact form and our team will reach out to discuss collaboration opportunities.
