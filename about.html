Our Story

Founded on Hope & Purpose

Trenchkid Support Foundation (TSF) was established by a group of passionate young Ghanaians who grew up watching talent wither due to poverty. They saw brilliant children fall through the cracks of a system that wasn't built for them.

TSF was born as a direct response — a community-driven movement to ensure that no child's future is determined by the circumstances of their birth.

Today, TSF empowers underprivileged children with education, digital skills, and mentorship, enabling them to build independent and impactful futures.

Our Journey

How TSF Grew

From a small idea to a movement touching hundreds of lives across Ghana.

🌱
2019 — The Beginning

TSF is Founded

A group of passionate youth in Bolga establish Trenchkid Support Foundation with the vision to break the cycle of poverty through education and digital empowerment.

📚
2020 — First Programs

First 50 Children Supported

TSF launches its inaugural school-fee support and mentorship program, directly sponsoring 50 children across 4 communities in Upper East Region.

💻
2021 — Going Digital

Digital Skills Initiative Launched

With a generous partnership, TSF introduces its Digital Skills Academy — teaching coding, graphic design, and computer literacy to 120+ youth.

🌍
2023 — Scaling Up

1,000+ Lives Touched

TSF surpasses the 1,000 children milestone, expands into new regions, and establishes partnerships with tech companies and international donors.

⭐
2025 — Today

Growing Every Day

TSF continues to expand its reach, with active programs in education, health, digital skills, and community development across Ghana.
