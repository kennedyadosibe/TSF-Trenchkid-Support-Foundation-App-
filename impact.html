Our Programs

What We Do

Education

School Support Program

We sponsor school fees, uniforms, books, and stationery for children from families who cannot afford them, keeping 600+ children in school annually.

📚 600+ children kept in school per year
Technology

Digital Skills Academy

Our intensive digital training covers coding, graphic design, computer literacy, and entrepreneurship — giving youth a competitive edge in the digital economy.

💻 380+ graduates trained
Mentorship

Youth Mentorship Program

We pair children with mentors from industry — developers, doctors, teachers, and entrepreneurs — who guide them through education and into career readiness.

🤝 250+ mentor-mentee pairs active
