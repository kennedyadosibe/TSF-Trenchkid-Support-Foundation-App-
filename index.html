About TSF

Breaking Cycles, Building Futures

Trenchkid Support Foundation was born out of a deep belief: that every child, regardless of where they come from, deserves access to quality education, digital skills, and the mentorship to succeed.

We don't give handouts — we give futures. Through targeted programs, community partnerships, and passionate volunteers, TSF transforms lives one child at a time.

Our Mission

What We Stand For

Four pillars guide every decision we make and every life we touch.

🔓

Breaking Poverty Cycles

We tackle the root causes of poverty by investing in children's potential — not just their immediate needs — creating lasting change for whole families.

🎓

Education & Skills

From school fees to digital literacy programs, we equip children with the tools and knowledge to compete in tomorrow's world.

🌟

Future Leaders

We don't just help kids survive — we help them become developers, entrepreneurs, community leaders, and agents of change.

🤲

Community That Cares

TSF mobilises volunteers, partners with schools and tech communities to create a network of support that outlives any single program.

🏥

Health & Wellbeing

A healthy child learns better. We support access to basic healthcare so that illness never becomes a barrier to education.

🌍

Measurable Impact

We track every child we support, every project we fund, and every community we reach — because accountability matters.
